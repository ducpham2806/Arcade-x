# Offline lexicon pack 1

Downloaded 2026-10-11. 117,597 distinct uppercase entries (3–10 ASCII letters), including 7,321 common entries. Target words remain English. Entries are word forms, not distinct meanings.

Primary redistributable source: https://github.com/dwyl/english-words (`words_alpha.txt`, Unlicense, see LICENSE.txt). Source SHA-256: 3ed0c94610d8bcf7c11bbb49c56aa49c7234d32b66824df91f554169e572da48.

Selection/frequency references: https://github.com/dolph/dictionary (`enable1.txt`) to exclude many abbreviations/noisy forms; https://github.com/first20hours/google-10000-english (`google-10000-english-no-swears.txt`) to identify common word forms. Pack contains selected word strings from the Unlicense source, not definitions or copied articles. An additional explicit-term filter is applied; this is not comprehensive linguistic/content moderation.

Levels 1–120 use the common subset; advanced levels 121–200 open the extended library and may contain unfamiliar inflections. The full pack is bundled and precached for offline play. Existing hand-authored definitions cover a small subset; missing definitions are explicitly labeled. Speech availability depends on the device's offline voices.
