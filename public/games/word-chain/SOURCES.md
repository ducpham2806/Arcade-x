# Từ điển Nối Từ (`words.v1.json`)

The file has 17,522 two-syllable Vietnamese words, sorted from most to least common, plus 3,011 opening words. It is generated by `node scripts/word-chain/build-lexicon.mjs`. Running the script with `--check` fails if the committed file no longer matches what the script produces.

**Source.** The words come from npm package `which-dialect-vi@0.2.0` (https://www.npmjs.com/package/which-dialect-vi), tarball SHA-1 `a53d8b87a204666cc5f020d744089ab8b35c6925`. That package takes its Vietnamese entries from [Wiktionary](https://en.wiktionary.org/), using [Kaikki.org](https://kaikki.org/dictionary/Vietnamese/)'s wiktextract extraction retrieved 2026-10-02. Word frequencies come from [wordfreq](https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq) by Robyn Speer (https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7199437). Both sources are licensed under **CC BY-SA 4.0** (see `LICENSE.txt`).

**Changes made here.** The script keeps only lowercase headwords of exactly two Vietnamese syllables. It then removes:
- proper names;
- "misspelling of" entries;
- words with a vulgar, sexual or slur sense, or containing a blocked syllable. This filter is a simple keyword check and does not replace a full content review.

Spellings that differ only in where the tone mark sits are merged into one word. This adapted list is shared under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 licence.

**Opening words.** An opening word must be a common noun (wordfreq score of 3 or more) made of two different syllables, and its last syllable must begin at least 12 words in the list. As a result, the first word of a match is never a dead end.
