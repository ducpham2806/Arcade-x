# Content Creator

A skill for crafting compelling, high-converting, and audience-first content across digital platforms and marketing channels.

## When to Use

- Writing persuasive marketing copy, landing page copy, or email newsletters
- Developing multi-channel content strategies and editorial calendars
- Crafting long-form guides, tutorials, case studies, and thought leadership articles
- Writing viral hooks and social media threads (LinkedIn, X/Twitter, Threads)
- Repurposing pillar content into snackable social posts, infographics, and snippets

## Content Creation Workflow

1. **Audience & Intent**: Identify target ICP, their pain points, and what action they should take after reading.
2. **Hook & Narrative**: Lead with a strong, curiosity-driven or contrarian opening that hooks attention immediately.
3. **Actionable Substance**: Deliver dense value, concrete examples, and frameworks with zero filler.
4. **Clear Call-to-Action (CTA)**: Single, low-friction next step aligned with the reader's intent.
